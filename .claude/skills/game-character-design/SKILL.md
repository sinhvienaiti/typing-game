# Character design for every game

On 01/11/2026 two hero designs in a row came out plain and villain-like because prompts were written before any research, and a third painted a halo ring into the picture. The owner: "đáng lẽ trước khi thiết kế nhân vật, bạn phải đi tìm các tài liệu, đọc và phân tích những nhân vật thần thoại hoặc truyền thuyết hoặc tu tiên, hoàng gia, chiến binh". Appeal is the goal (`AGENTS.md`): a character that is correct but plain, simple or generic fails 8.5.

This applies to every game in this repo (Dragonhold, Wordwoven, Last Line, Tu Tien, Space Typing) and any later game; game names below are examples only. The game's own design docs decide who the character is; this skill decides how it is designed and accepted. General art rules (tools, set standard, judging, Tripo) live in [game-art-pipeline](../game-art-pipeline/SKILL.md); this skill adds what is specific to characters.

1. **Frame the job.** Role, story function, what the player does with it, which camera sees it and from which side. Names the player reads are English; unique characters keep their proper names. For a foe or boss, search `docs/games/MONSTER_MODEL_INVENTORY.md` first and reuse (retexture, recolour, rescale, re-rig); a new monster needs a written reason.
2. **Measure the play view before drawing.** On-screen height at 1280×720 and 1920×1080 from camera pitch, FOV and the character's height; the side the camera sees most; the map's ground values; the colours the game reserves for signals. Put these numbers at the top of the brief.
3. **Research with sources** ([research lenses](references/research-lenses.md)). Search yourself and ask ChatGPT as a second opinion: a URL behind each claim, then open the sources and mark each key claim verified, partly verified, unsupported or wrong; add what it missed and say where you disagree. Never accept it unchecked. Keep a research note with links next to the brief.
4. **Write the brief against the [appeal checklist](references/appeal-checklist.md).** Every item gets a concrete choice (a shape, a named material, a colour with its share), not adjectives. Every prompt asks for captivating, stylish, luxurious, fashionable, modern design with rich layered detail.
5. **Plan the radiance kit** (halo or mandorla, emissive channels, light motes or orbiting ornaments, a cape or ribbons in motion). What is model geometry and what is engine VFX comes from [the pipeline decision](references/pipeline-decision.md), never from a guess. Never cram the whole kit into one image and then redo that image forever.
6. **Show the owner 2–3 directions** before any turnaround, Tripo source or key UI art ([directions](references/views-and-sources.md#directions-for-the-owner)). Build only on the one the owner picks.
7. **Make the sources** ([views and sources](references/views-and-sources.md)): a turnaround sheet first, then each view in the same ChatGPT chat (Chat mode, High, never Work). When ChatGPT hits its image limit, redo the whole set in one Gemini chat; never mix tools inside one set. Firefly cannot hold one design across views. Flat even light, no painted glow, rig-ready pose, keyed background.
8. **Check before judging.** Consistency across views (detail list, landmark heights, palette), a back view that really is a back, and a check tile at the real on-screen size from the play camera. Sheets go under the game's `tmp/`; prune your own raw frames.
9. **Judge blind:** two independent judges, both ≥ 8.5, on the [criteria](references/views-and-sources.md#judging), including appeal, market level and view consistency. Under 8.5, fix the named fault. After two rounds without a real gain, change approach (more research, references, ready-made sources, another tool), not more rerolls.
10. **Then 3D and the game.** Tripo through [game-art-pipeline](../game-art-pipeline/SKILL.md) (Space Typing's runtime import stays in [game-asset-2d-to-3d](../game-asset-2d-to-3d/SKILL.md)), with the living numbers in `docs/games/LESSONS_FROM_THREE_GAMES.md` §4 and `docs/games/MODEL_QUALITY.md`. Rig and clips are ours, never Tripo's auto rig (`docs/games/CHARACTER_ANIMATION.md` §5.3). Radiance VFX follows the bloom and budget rules of `docs/games/VFX_STANDARD.md`. Final grade in the built play host, player settings, no URL flags; character animation is a shared system judged at 9, and the owner signs off last.

Read [the failures we hit](references/failures.md) before starting. A worked example (Last Line heroine and drone) is in [worked examples](references/worked-examples.md).
> **Not for skill creatures** (owner, 05/11/2026). A dragon, beast or spirit summoned by a skill is an energy effect, not a character: translucent fire, qi or light, soft detail. Make it with [game-vfx-production](../game-vfx-production/SKILL.md), not with this skill's four views and Tripo.
