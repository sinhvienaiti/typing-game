## Seven improvements (layout kept)

1. **The West Warden grows out of the cliff.** The wing roots sink into the rock, the talons merge with cracked ledges, and the erosion lines run on from cliff to sculpture. Its eye basins stay dead and black, so the living fire dragon keeps the strongest fire.
2. **One masonry language for the Hold:** huge fitted granite blocks, dark iron cramps, worn chamfers, drainage grooves, lichen in sheltered joints, patches of repaired masonry. The bridge is older and wetter; the rampart is heavier.
3. **The two lanes differ by material.** Bridge Lane is engineered: granite flags with wheel wear. Brook Lane is primitive: packed earth, gravel, damp footprints, stepping stones. Both stay brighter than the grass.
4. **The river adds depth, not spectacle:** dark slate-teal water, gravel in the shallows, and foam only at the piers, the cascade and the stepping stones. Highlights stay narrow, never a turquoise stripe.
5. **A thin threat at the north gate:** a low, thin violet haze. Goblins, the boar and the ant swarms half fade into the pine shadow; only their eyes show as tiny yellow-green points.
6. **A simpler right wing:** the cascade is a pale vertical rhythm, partly hidden by pines; no extra structures.
7. **The rampart frames the foreground:** a heavy dark band at the bottom, slightly oversized battlements, dark pine crowns in the corners.
