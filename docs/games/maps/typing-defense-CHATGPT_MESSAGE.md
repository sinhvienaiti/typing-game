You are a senior art director at a studio shipping commercial top-down strategy games. I need a blunt review of one battle-map concept for our game Wordwoven, and then one concept painting made over a real frame of the game.

## (a) What to do

1. **Review the map 1 concept in (c)** against the commercial bar in (b):
   - score it 0–10, where 9 is a top commercial key frame and 8.5 is our acceptance bar;
   - list the gaps: scale, depth, light, colour script, focal points, readability, life;
   - propose concrete improvements that **keep the gameplay layout and the camera exactly as they are**. Roads, the stream, the plazas, the houses and the HUD do not move. Say what you would add, change or remove, where (use the screen positions given), and why.
2. **Paint one concept image over the attached base image**, with your improvements in it:
   - keep the exact camera, perspective and layout: every road edge, both plazas, the stream, every house, foe and soldier in place and at its size;
   - each translucent coloured block marks where a planned element goes; replace it with that element (legend in (c)). The colours are placement codes, not colours to paint;
   - keep the grey HUD boxes flat grey;
   - 16:9, 1280 × 720 or larger at the same framing; **no text, no sky, no horizon**.
3. **Score your painting** against the same bar. Below 8.5, say what is missing and paint again, at most three tries in all.
4. Give the other maps in (d) a quick idea review, one or two lines each.

## (b) Hard constraints

**The bar.**
- The references are commercial, not our own style:
  - a near-photoreal fantasy frame: a colossal flying beast over a sunlit canyon, a cloaked hero, cinematic light, huge scale contrast;
  - our accepted title painting: sheer karst towers in mist, silver falls, a jade river with lanterns, terraces, a sky turtle carrying ruins.
- Keywords: epic, majestic, cinematic light, deep atmosphere, painterly-realistic, bold readable shapes.
- Our chibi-chunky models (Fort, towers, foes, soldiers) stay as they are. The world around them must reach the bar.

**Camera.**
- The base is the game's real battle camera: 42° tilt, 46 m from the look point, 42° vertical lens, looking north.
- About 11.7 px per metre at the top edge and 29 px per metre at the bottom (720p); a 2 m foe is 24–32 px tall.
- At this tilt the top edge looks 21° below the horizon, so **the player never sees the sky in battle**. Paint no horizon, no sky and no far mountains above the ground.
- **Height budget.** A standing thing whose foot is in the top band shows very little before the frame cuts it:

  | Its foot at screen y | 20 | 45 | 70 | 90 | 135 | 180 | 360 | 540 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Height in frame (m) | 1.6 | 3.5 | 5.2 | 6.5 | 9 | 11 | 18 | 22 |

  - Tall things near the top edge are cropped: trees there show trunks and the lowest boughs, crowns overhanging from beyond the edge.
  - Nothing over 3 m may stand in front of (below on screen) a road or build ground, because it would hide play.
  - Scale must come from what lies on the ground, what is cropped by the top edge, water, light, giant shadows and life.

**Readability.**
- The brightest things on screen will be the word cards the game draws over houses and foes (ivory and gold), typed letters and shots. Keep the ground under houses calm and below 60 % brightness.
- A grey, blurred copy must still show the roads, foes, soldiers and health bars.
- Roads read at least a clear step brighter than the grass.

**Colours reserved for signals.**
- Ivory and gold glow, warm white-gold flashes, cold cyan (the Ghost Flame wisps), and the Rift's indigo-black with a cold rim.
- The world uses none of these at full strength.
- No bright pink, magenta or purple anywhere.
- Shadows are jade and teal, never black; light is warm gold; water and mist are silver; stone is limestone white; accents are small cinnabar red and lantern amber; grass is jade-teal, not mint.

**Content rules.**
- English game; no text in the image.
- No religious buildings or objects: no pagodas, temples, shrines, torii, altars, statues of deities.
- No thatched huts, bamboo walls, windmills, rainbows, cartoon flowers or candy-floss trees.
- The four houses in the base are the current models and keep their silhouettes. You may paint their roofs as dark fired tile or stone instead of straw if that reaches the bar better; say which you chose.

**Buildability.** We build the result from our own models, free commercial-safe assets (CC0 rocks, ground and tree materials, MIT shaders) and VFX. Ask for a bespoke 3D model only if it is clearly worth one.

## (c) Map 1 concept, condensed: Silverfall Meadow

**Story.**
- A high meadow on the brink of the Silverfall gorge, where the Silver Brook runs to the falls.
- At dusk an ink-black Rift tears open at the meadow's north edge, just beyond the top of the frame, and its ink runs down the old road.
- The village must hold five nights until the nameless banyan spirit, Mộc Tinh, rises and is driven back.

**What the frame shows** (screen positions at 1280 × 720):
- The old flagstone road enters at the top edge (x 480–560), splits at the fork plaza (678, 44), loops round the meadow (bends at (417, 131) and (833, 131)) and meets again at the merge plaza (568, 504).
- It leaves the bottom edge at (387, 680) toward the Fort, which stands just below the frame.
- The Silver Brook runs down the left third.
- Houses: Archer Tower (378, 504), Cannon Tower (735, 504), Barracks (640, 630), Gold Mine (946, 66).
- Foes walk down from the top; three spearmen hold the merge.

**Moment to paint:** dusk of the first night, the Rift's ink spilling, the defence ready.

**Light and mood.**
- Low golden sun from the lower left (south-west), long sharp shadows reaching to the upper right, warm rim light on stone and on the figures.
- Cool jade-teal in every shadow.
- Lanterns just lit on the towers.
- Cumulus shadows drifting across the meadow.
- The ink's cold glow at the top edge is the only cold light, besides any Ghost Flame wisps.

**Materials.** Wet fitted limestone flagstones with worn verges and moss in the joints; a jade-teal meadow with moss patches and wildflowers in drifts, not dots; limestone boulders at grove edges; foam at the cascades; gold seams catching the low sun.

**What carries the scale** (nothing tall may stand in the play): the ink tide, the cropped forest edge, the spur leaving the frame, the 16 m ring, the gorge mist, giant cloud shadows, and the life (Draconis' sweeping shadow, the waiting pack, the spearmen's line).

**Today's frame, for honesty.** One flat mint-green lawn, a beige road, a grey straight gutter for a stream, flower decals, no trees drawn, an empty loop centre, thatched houses.

## (d) The other maps, for a quick idea review

- **Whitestone Bay:** two Rifts on a white beach while the tide turns every two waves; seen from above, surf rolls through the arches of a colossal sea gate whose roofs leave the frame; the Storm Eagle's shadow sweeps the sand.

- **Starsea Rift:** floating basalt islands over a cloud sea, joined by light bridges that switch by wave, lit by star-fall from a tear in the sky.
