## B. Hard constraints

- **Camera.** High strategy camera looking 55° down, 30° vertical field of view, 95 m from the look point, facing north. The base image is a real capture from it. Do not move, tilt or widen it; no side view, diorama or floating island.
- **No sky, no horizon.** The game camera never sees the sky at any allowed tilt: the horizon is far above the top edge. Land continues past every edge of the frame. No mountains silhouetted against the sky, no clouds above a skyline.
- **Gameplay reads first.**
  - The lanes are the clearest ground in the frame: worn granite flags on the bridge road, packed earth on the ford road, clearly brighter than the grass around them.
  - Ground under where word tags float (the lanes and the area around the platform) stays mid-dark: no snow, pale stone or sunlit water there.
  - Nothing tall stands between the camera and a lane. Tall pieces lean outward toward the frame edges, away from play.
  - One hero landmark. Everything else frames it and must not compete with it.
- **Colours reserved for signals.** Never paint large areas in bright red, orange, gold or turquoise. Those colours are the game's warning and reward signals. Blight yellow-green appears only as small points: foe eyes and cracks. Fire orange only in the braziers and on the dragon.
- **Style.** Painterly-realistic: real material response (wet stone, lichen, bark, needles, cloth, iron) with a painterly colour grade, cinematic light, epic scale. Not cute, not cozy, not toy-like, not plastic, not low-poly.
- **Only what we can build.** Every piece must come from:
  - our six landmark models (stone dragon statue, curtain wall, watchtower, ruined arch, brazier, gatehouse);
  - free commercial-safe scanned rocks, pines and textures;
  - terrain, water and particle shaders;
  - our existing creatures: the dragons, small moss goblins with stone knives, moss archers, grey wolves, tusked boars, thorn wasps, thorn ants, a moss goblin standard-bearer, and on our side moss folk with bark shields (Grove Wardens).
  - Do not add other creatures, animals or birds, people in armour, ships, temples, shrines or statues of gods.

## C. The concept: Map 1, "Bridge and Brook"

**Story.** Dawn in a mountain pass. The blight comes down the valley to the north face of the Hold, an ancient granite fortress just below the frame. The fire dragon holds the two river crossings beneath the West Warden, a colossal stone dragon carved into the cliff, which has watched this pass for a thousand years.

**Layout in the base image (fixed).**
- The **Dragon Platform** is at the centre: a round stone dais in the capture, to be painted as an octagonal granite bastion with stairs north, west and south.
  - A large red-orange **fire dragon** hovers over it.
  - A smaller **pearl-white dragon** stands at its south-west edge.
- The **Bridge Lane** comes from the top centre-right, out of a ruined arch in the forest, crosses the river on the bridge and reaches the platform's north stair. The bridge is the choke point.
- The **Brook Lane** comes in from the left, out of a cleft below the cliff, wades the stream at a ford of stepping stones and reaches the platform's west stair.
- A **river** enters at the top right, crosses the upper third under the bridge, bends down the left side past the ford and leaves at the bottom-left corner.
- **Thorn wasps** fly in low from the right, over the east meadow.
- In the capture, small green moss goblins walk both lanes, and a dark swarm of thorn ants sits at the bridge's north end.
- Today's scenery in the capture (round cartoon trees, sand paths, a garden pond on the right, a plank bridge) is what we are replacing. Use it only for positions.

**Depth.**
- Foreground: the rampart top, braziers, banners (ember cloth, dark iron dragon sigil, no letters), dark pine crowns past the wall ends, in shadow.
- Playfield: olive meadow with gold sun patches, granite outcrops, the river in a real bed with gravel bars, boulders and foam.
- Wings: the cliff and the Warden on the left; a forested ridge with the cascade and one blighted dead pine where the wasps gather on the right.
- Top band: a dark pine forest wall climbing north, violet mist at the north gate, the muster ground.

**Light and colour.**
- Dawn, sun low from the east-north-east (upper right), about 24° high; long shadows fall toward the lower left.
- Mist lies 0–3 m deep in the river bed; spray hangs at the cascade; light shafts through the mist; one soft cloud shadow crossing the meadow.
- Shadows deep pine green and slate blue; light dawn gold on granite and grass tips; granite cream; river slate-teal with white foam, not turquoise; blight muted violet at the gates only.
- The Warden is the largest lit mass but not the brightest; the dragon's fire and the braziers are the brightest points.

**Life.** The map must feel inhabited and about to be attacked: the waiting muster in the shade, the defenders on the walls and the platform stairs, the two dragons, smoke rising from behind the rampart, banners and pines moving in one wind. No birds or other animals.

**Grey boxes.** The status bar (top left), system buttons (top right), dragon panel (bottom left), typing bar (bottom centre) and item strip (bottom right) are game interface. Keep them grey and empty.
