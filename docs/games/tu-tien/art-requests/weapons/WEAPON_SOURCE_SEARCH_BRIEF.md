# Asset search brief: ready-made xianxia weapons (02/11/2026)

Written for ChatGPT (or any search assistant) and for Claude's research agents. Search the web and return candidates in the table at the end. Do not buy or download anything; the owner decides. Owner, 02/11: "bạn đã kêu chatgpt tìm kiếm source hay thư viện có thể lấy về dùng mà không vi phạm chưa, chứ tôi thấy bên trung rất nhiều hình kiếm đẹp".

## The game

Tu Tien (English title pending): a premium xianxia (Chinese immortal cultivation) typing-defence game in three.js, shipped as a web build, sold commercially. Fixed camera 55° from above. The equipped weapon is the shape source of every skill (canon D55): 3, 6, up to 500 flying copies of it. Style (canon D56): anime, donghua and mythic spectacle, radiant and luxurious, not realistic, not low-poly toy.

## What we need

10 weapons in one set: 4 straight swords (jian), 3 sabres (dao: yanmaodao, niuweidao or zhanmadao, liuyedao), 3 polearms (qiang spear, ji with one crescent side blade, ji with two side blades). Materials of the set: satin silver, white porcelain, small pale jade accents, indigo silk grips. One glowing channel per weapon.

Useful candidates, in order:
1. **3D models** (GLB, glTF, FBX or OBJ) of Chinese swords, sabres, spears, ji, stylized or high-quality realistic, that we can restyle or use as a base mesh: 5k–50k triangles, clean UVs, PBR textures.
2. **Museum photos and scans of real Chinese weapons** under CC0 (The Met Open Access, Smithsonian Open Access, Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access, Rijksmuseum, other CC0 museum collections): fittings, guards, pommels, spearheads, ji, for reference and as inputs to image models.
3. **2D weapon art or icon packs** in an East Asian fantasy style with a licence that allows commercial game use.
4. **Chinese sites**: search Chinese-language sources too, for example 爱给网 (aigei.com), 站酷 (zcool), 3D溜溜, CG模型网, 模型网, 新片场素材, 花瓣, Bilibili or Gitee repos, and Chinese artists on Sketchfab and ArtStation. Most beautiful sword art on Chinese sites is game art or ripped models: those are banned. Return only items whose own page states a licence that meets the rules below.

## Licence rules (hard requirements)

- Allowed: **CC0**; **CC-BY** (we credit on the Credits page); free custom licences that allow commercial game use with no payment and forbid only reselling the asset as a pack (for example Quaternius QAL).
- Banned: CC NC, ND or SA; GPL family when modified; "free" or "royalty-free" without a clear licence; personal or editorial use only; Sketchfab or Fab **Standard**; Unity Asset Store EULA; Mixamo; anything tagged **NoAI**; assets ripped or taken from other games; paid content.
- Give the exact licence name and the link to the licence on the **asset's own page**. We verify every candidate on its own page before use (FREE_ASSET_SOURCES rules).
- Engine-agnostic: usable in a custom WebGL engine.
