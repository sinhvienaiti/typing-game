# Skill staging study: Naraka: Bladepoint and Diablo IV (17/11/2026)

## Summary (under 300 words)

The owner chose Naraka: Bladepoint and Diablo IV as references for our 14 ultimates, which all currently "rise from the ground and form a ring".

Findings:
1. **Origin varies by skill, not by element.** Both games stage the same family of skills (fire, ice, lightning) from different sources: sky (Meteor, Blizzard), caster (Flame Shield, ribbons), ground (Hydra, Inferno), side or projectile (Fireball, Chain Lightning, Yoto slashes).
2. **One accent per skill.** Diablo IV is dark and desaturated, so a single warm or cold pool of light is the accent. Naraka is brighter, but each hero still has one signature (Ziping jade silk, Yoto blade arc).
3. **Readability from a high camera** comes from silhouette and light on a dark floor: a bright core, a thin pale support layer, and the ground lit by the effect. Rings are rare and always secondary.
4. **A skill is a story in time:** a 0.3-1.0 s telegraph, a sharp release, a travel phase with a trail, a heavy impact (hit-stop, flash, shake) and 1-4 s of aftermath (burning ground, lingering field).
5. **Foes react** with knock-up, stagger, freeze, pull or burn tint, not just damage numbers.
6. **Shapes are built from reusable parts:** one hero mesh or flipbook, ground decals, particles, trails.

Recommendation: give each element a different source type. At most 2 elements share one. The mapping table at the end proposes this, with Metal as the owner's example (formation circle, then a sword rain from the sky).

**Method note and honesty.** Ads and a small browser pane limited frame study. Frames were actually viewed for Sorcerer (Arc Lash, Fireball, Flame Shield, Ball Lightning, Meteor) and Naraka (Ziping Yin, Yoto Hime, Feria Shen). Everything else is written from the showcase videos' titles plus the author's knowledge of the games, and all timings are estimates (marked "est."). Re-check against the video before building. Screenshots were for viewing only and were deleted. No frame went to any AI tool.

## Skill notes

Format: source / motion / phases (est.) / accent / foes / top-down read / built from.

### Diablo IV, Sorcerer
1. **Meteor** (viewed). Sky. A glowing rock falls at an angle onto a marked spot. Charge 0.3 s (target glow), travel 0.6 s, impact 0.1 s, aftermath 3-4 s burning ground. Accent: the fireball's orange core. Foes: knock-up, burn. Top-down: excellent, the diagonal streak reads as a line toward the target. Built from: mesh plus trail, shock flash, crater decal, ember particles, fire flipbook.
2. **Blizzard.** Sky area. Ice shards fall across a large circle for about 6 s. No big release. Accent: pale blue sparkle. Foes: chill, slow. Top-down: a soft blue field, readable by the dark floor around it. Built from: area decal, falling-shard particles, frost mist, ground frost.
3. **Chain Lightning.** Projectile / side. A bolt jumps from the caster to the first foe and on to others. Charge 0.1 s, travel 0.15 s per jump, a few jumps. Accent: bright white-blue core. Foes: stagger. Top-down: lines between foes read clearly. Built from: beam mesh, jagged flipbook, impact sparks.
4. **Ball Lightning** (viewed). Projectile, slow. A glowing orb travels and zaps nearby foes. Travel 2-3 s. Accent: the orb. Top-down: a moving point of light with small arcs. Built from: sprite, arc beams, small particles.
5. **Inferno.** Ground (a fire serpent from the caster). A fire snake rises and tracks foes for several seconds. Charge 0.4 s, active 5-6 s. Accent: the serpent's head. Top-down: a sinuous long shape, strong silhouette. Built from: skinned or ribbon mesh, fire flipbook, heat distortion, scorch decal.
6. **Hydra.** Ground summon. Several heads rise and spit fire. Charge 0.5 s, active 8-10 s. Accent: the heads (3D forms). Top-down: static summoned objects, readable as mini bosses. Built from: skinned mesh, projectile fire, fire decal.
7. **Fireball** (viewed). Projectile. Orange orb flies, explodes on contact. Travel 0.3 s, impact 0.1 s, a short burn. Accent: the orb. Built from: sprite plus trail, burst flipbook, small decal.
8. **Flame Shield** (viewed). Caster-centred. A ring of fire hugs the caster, a tight orange loop at the feet. Duration 3-4 s. Accent: the orange loop. Top-down: reads as a halo on the ground, small, which is why it works. Built from: ring mesh or ribbon, ember particles.
9. **Arc Lash** (viewed). Caster to front. A whip of lightning leaves the caster and drags foes in. Release 0.2 s. Accent: the whip. Foes: pulled in. Built from: beam mesh, spark particles.

### Diablo IV, Druid, Necromancer, Barbarian, Spiritborn
10. **Cataclysm (Druid ultimate).** Sky and area. A storm cell forms over the caster. Lightning and a tornado strike around. Charge 0.5 s, active 8 s. Accent: the lightning strikes. Foes: stagger, lightning damage. Built from: cloud card, bolt flipbooks, tornado mesh, ground decal.
11. **Hurricane.** Caster-centred area. A wind ring and debris rotate around the caster for 6-8 s. Accent: the rotating cone. Built from: scrolled-texture cylinder, leaf and debris particles.
12. **Grizzly Rage.** Transformation, no ground effect. The caster becomes a bear for 15 s. Charge 0.4 s (flash), then a persistent aura and altered silhouette. Accent: the silhouette change. Built from: model swap, flash, aura.
13. **Blood Surge (Necromancer).** Caster-centred. Blood orbs spin out, burst outward and heal. Release 0.3 s, 2 s of bursts. Accent: dark red orbs, drawn from foes. Foes: bleed. Built from: orb meshes, trail ribbons, impact bursts.
14. **Bone Storm.** Caster-centred. Bone shards spin around the caster in a cylinder, damaging. 4-6 s. Accent: the bone ring. Top-down: reads as a moving ring. Built from: ring of meshes, particles.
15. **Call of the Ancients (Barbarian).** Side / ground summon. Three ancient warriors appear next to the caster and fight. Charge 0.3 s, active 6 s. Accent: the ancients (3D, glowing). Foes: engaged by the allies. Built from: skinned meshes, spawn flash, ground dust.
16. **Spiritborn Soar / Rushing Claw / Thunderspike (est.).** Caster to target. A spirit animal form launches, a trail of light, an impact burst. Travel 0.3 s. Accent: the animal glow. Built from: mesh, trail, burst.

### Naraka: Bladepoint
17. **Ziping Yin** (viewed). Caster-centred. Wide jade-green silk ribbons wrap and swirl around the heroine in the air, with petals and glints. Release 0.3 s, active 2-3 s. Accent: the broad jade ribbons. Top-down: large translucent sweeps, readable by colour and curve. Built from: ribbon meshes (animated UV), particles, glow rims.
18. **Yoto Hime** (viewed). Sky to ground / front. A violet and gold blade arc sweeps a long line in front of the caster. Charge 0.4 s, release 0.15 s, aftermath 0.5 s. Accent: the sharp bright blade edge. Built from: crescent mesh, trail, spark flipbook.
19. **Feria Shen** (viewed). Ground at the front. A mechanical arm fires a flame cone along the floor, hits sparks. Active 2 s. Accent: the orange cone. Built from: cone mesh or flipbook, ground scorch decal.
20. **Tianhai (est.).** Caster to front, a charging strike with a speed streak, dust and a dust ring. Accent: the streak. Foes: knocked back. Built from: dust ring, streak mesh, shock-wave decal.
21. **Viper Ning, Twilight Crimson (est.).** Caster-centred, a crimson storm of petals / blades around the caster with a fading red trail. Active 3-4 s. Accent: red blades. Built from: orbiting meshes, ribbons.
22. **Tarka Ji, Fire Frenzy (est.).** Forward run with a fire body, knocking foes aside and blocking ranged hits. Accent: the fire silhouette. Built from: fire flipbook on body, dust, shock decal.
23. **Kurumi (est.).** Front slashes with elegant cuts plus a spreading afterimage. Accent: the slash line. Built from: curved ribbons, glints.
24. **Valda Cui (est.).** Ground burst of spiked energy around the caster. Charge 0.3 s. Accent: the spikes. Built from: instanced spikes, decal.
25. **Justina Gu (est.).** Caster area, a dispersing healing pulse with light motes. Accent: the pulse. Built from: sprites, rings, motes.
26. **Temulch ultimate (est.).** Summoned giant entity in front of the caster that blocks ranged attacks. Accent: its glowing eyes and silhouette. Built from: skinned mesh, spawn flash, dust.

## Patterns that apply to us

- Variety of source: sky, caster, ground, front, summon.
- A fixed order: telegraph, release, travel, impact, aftermath. Our rings are all "release" with no telegraph and no travel.
- Rings only as telegraph or aftermath marks, never as the skill.
- Light on the floor (decals, a coloured pool) is what makes a skill readable from above.
- Summons (Hydra, Ancients, Temulch) give a long, readable presence and a new silhouette.

## Mapping: our 14 elements

Source-type count (max 2 each): sky 2 (Metal, Star); ground 2 (Wood, Earth); caster-centred 2 (Blood, Poison); projectile 2 (Thunder, Dark); side sweep or slash 2 (Water, Wind); summon or creature 2 (Fire, Void); area storm 1 (Ice); beam 1 (Light).

Note for builders: Metal and Star both use the sky. Keep their differences strong (Metal: blades in vertical rain, a formation circle; Star: diagonal meteors, a constellation, no circle). Fire and Wood both use creatures from the ground side; keep one a trail-maker (Fire) and the other a stationary lasher (Wood).
