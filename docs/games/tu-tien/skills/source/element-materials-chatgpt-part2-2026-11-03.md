5. WOOD — Life and Growth

Fantasy: The weapon does not merely travel; it germinates, grows, branches and claims space.

Law applied to choreography: Wood attacks should grow into their trajectory. Sword formations sprout additional blades like branches; Sabre sweeps unfurl; Spear thrusts elongate like impossibly fast-growing thorns.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	Living spiritwood/jade blades: dark lacquered heartwood, translucent celadon edges, bright sap-veins; small leaves only as secondary breakup
Sabre	A thick branch/frond crescent growing along the swing path, with a pale living edge and layered bark/wood body
Spear	A rapidly elongating spirit-thorn / bamboo-like lance, narrow at first, then extending segment-by-segment toward the target

6. EARTH — Mass and Substance

Fantasy: The cultivator weaponizes the battlefield's physical mass.

Law applied to choreography: Earth should obey weight before speed. Matter gathers, compresses, hangs for a perceptible fraction, then moves with crushing inertia. Even fast Spear attacks should feel like a mountain being accelerated rather than a beam.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	Thick stone/ore swords, chipped carved faces with bright mineral seams; large skills summon monumental lithic blades
Sabre	A huge tectonic slab-crescent, almost like a moving cliff face with a sharp compressed leading plane
Spear	A narrow stone/ore battering lance, compact rock at the nose with a dense conical compression wake

Avoid floating orange crystals as the primary material. It should read as actual substance.

Five phases
Phase	Material / shape	Ramp, core → edge	Motion
Charge	Pebbles, stone plates and dust lift from ground; concentric compression cracks	#FFF6DD → #D8BE8B → #987754 → #4B4642	Matter rises reluctantly; pieces rotate slowly and pull inward
Release	Fragments violently lock into one dense weapon/slab	#FFF5D8 → #D3B57C → #8D6E50 → #44413F	Gather → compress → heavy drop/launch
Travel	Sandstone/granite/ore body; minimal glowing mineral seams	#F8EFD7 → #C6A571 → #80654D → #3E3C3B	Low, heavy trajectory; dust is dragged behind rather than sprayed everywhere
Impact	Ground visibly depresses before fragments erupt; chunky radial fracture	#FFF1CB → #C8A26A → #795B46 → #383635	Contact → compression → delayed upheaval
Aftermath	Real rubble, uplifted plates, cracked paving and settled dust	#C1AA84 → #88705A → #4F4741	Large pieces settle first; fine dust follows later

Only the fresh fracture/mineral seam gets HDR.

7. WIND — Atmosphere and Pressure

Fantasy: The weapon commands the air volume itself; enemies are struck by pressure they cannot see.

Law applied to choreography: Wind should displace the world before the weapon arrives. Dust, grass, cloth, leaves and loose particles announce a pressure front; then the visible weapon-shaped compression passes through.

Do not define Wind primarily by green ribbons.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	Almost transparent compressed-air blades, visible by thin pale rims, condensation streaks and debris caught along their boundaries
Sabre	A broad pressure crescent / shock front, thickest at its outer rim and almost empty inside
Spear	A narrow Mach-cone pressure lance, bright condensation collar around an otherwise nearly invisible vector

Impact	Air visibly dents inward, then erupts as a planar shock cone/ring	#FFFFFF → #D7FFF3 → #8BDCCB → #528F91	Suction → compression → outward pressure break
Aftermath	Moving dust sheets, leaves, cloth and fading vortices	#C6EEE6 → #7DB7AE → #586F70	Matter continues travelling after visible magic disappears

8. ICE — Stillness and Stopped Time

Fantasy: Ice does not merely make things cold; it arrests motion and preserves the instant of death.

Law applied to choreography: movement progressively loses degrees of freedom. Particles slow, arcs become straighter, enemies lock into poses, and the climax is often a moment of absolute stillness followed by one catastrophic fracture.

This should be the strongest contrast to Water.

Sabre	A broad crescent crystal plate, like a slice of frozen air with serrated frost growth along its wake

Part 2 lock — all eight laws
Element	Dominant material behaviour	Must remain visible even in grayscale
Metal	Alignment + hardness	Objects snap into exact formation → shear
Wood	Growth	Geometry elongates, branches and roots
Water	Flow + domain	Continuous mass wraps and keeps travelling
Fire	Compression + burst	Matter collapses inward → violent expansion
Earth	Mass	Heavy solid matter gathers → crushes → remains
Thunder	Judgment	Target marked → vertical answer from heaven
Wind	Atmospheric pressure	Environment moves before invisible pressure front
Ice	Stopped time	Motion arrests → held tableau → fracture

The most important separation in Part 2 is Water vs Ice and Metal vs Wind: Water must keep moving while Ice stops; Metal shows the projectile itself, while Wind is primarily revealed by what the air does to everything around it.
