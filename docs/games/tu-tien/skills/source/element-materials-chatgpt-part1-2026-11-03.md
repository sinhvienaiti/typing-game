Part 1 — Element Materials: Metal, Fire, Water, Thunder
Shared design rule

At 40–100 px, motion + silhouette must identify the element before texture detail does. Riot’s VFX guidance similarly prioritizes gameplay hierarchy, well-defined silhouettes, concise detail, shape language, anticipation → main event → dissipation, and lower-value aftermath. 
Riot Games
+2

Therefore the four kits need four unmistakable verbs:

Element	Primary verb	Instant read
Metal	ALIGN → SHEAR	precise hard blades snap into formation, then one clean cut
Fire	COMPRESS → BURST	everything sucks into a tiny white-gold core, then violently expands
Water	FLOW → ENGULF	continuous curved mass that keeps moving through/around targets
Thunder	MARK → JUDGE	weapon designates; power arrives vertically from heaven

For all ramps below, only the thin core should exceed bloom threshold; the larger body/halo remains ≤0.9.

1. METAL — Edge and Hardness

Fantasy: The weapon becomes absolute. Metal removes softness: trajectories straighten, formations lock into exact geometry, and every attack resolves as a decisive shear rather than an explosion.

Law applied to choreography: weapon motions align matter first, then cut. Sword swarms snap into ranks; Sabre arcs become rigid cutting planes; Spear thrusts become needle-straight penetrators.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	Keep the real Clear Wind jade/silver sword model; white-hot cutting edge + jade-cyan seam; massed skills use actual discrete swords, not cyan beams
Sabre	A broad polished silver metal-qi crescent, visually almost solid; thin white cutting rim, dark steel interior, sparse filings
Spear	A long silver needle/lance of condensed edge-force, tiny spearhead point followed by a rigid metallic pressure line
Five phases

Charge	Silver shards, jade splinters, thin blade silhouettes orbit caster; formation lines appear	#FFFFFF → #DDFBFF → #8AE0D1 → #758894	Loose shards progressively snap into identical orientation per typed letters
Release	Weapon edge collapses to a razor-white line; short star glint at tip	#FFFFFF → #D8FFFF → #75D7CC	1–2 frame lock, then instantaneous linear acceleration
Travel	Hard physical blade/plane; minimal trail; occasional steel glint	#F8FFFF → #BFE8EA → #7AAFAE → #65727D	Extremely straight; swarm trajectories run in parallel or exact curves, never turbulent
Impact	Thin planar cut + angular metal fragments; optional black/white manga cut frame for large skills	#FFFFFF → #C7FFFF → #80D9CD → #53616B	Contact → tiny pause → line propagates across target/ground → fragments eject perpendicular to cut
Aftermath	Embedded blades/fragments, clean glowing incision lines fading to dull steel	#BFFBF4 → #72B9B0 → #566067	No smoke cloud; fragments quiver once, then become still

2. FIRE — Compression, Then Burst

Fantasy: A furnace forced into a weapon until containment fails.

Law applied to choreography: fire should initially move against expectation: flames, embers and light are sucked inward. Only after maximum compression does the weapon erupt outward.

This makes Fire uniquely readable even if all three weapon families have different shapes.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	A compressed flame-forged sword: dark hot body wrapped tightly around a white-gold seam; trailing flame hugs blade instead of forming a random torch
Sabre	A thick crescent sheet of compressed furnace fire, white/gold leading edge, orange body, torn dark-red trailing fragments
Spear	A white-hot fire lance, narrow bright nose with a long pressure-flame wake; almost a supernatural cutting torch
Five phases
Phase	Material / shape	Ramp, core → edge	Motion
Charge	Ember particles + flame ribbons collapse into pea-sized furnace core around weapon/caster	#FFFBE2 → #FFE066 → #FF9A27 → #B92B12	Strong reverse flow inward; core gets smaller and brighter as typing progresses
Release	Tiny overbright seed surrounded by thin compressed flame shell	#FFFFFF → #FFF19A → #FFB12E → #E65B19	1-frame contraction → pressure-ring → projectile launches
Travel	Dense flame body, not fluffy fire; pointed/tapered weapon silhouette survives	#FFF9D2 → #FFD24A → #FF8423 → #B62615	Tight turbulent wake; sparks trail backward; projectile periodically contracts rather than continuously expanding
Impact	First white-gold pressure flash, then expanding orange flame petals/tongues	#FFFFFF → #FFE05D → #FF8A22 → #9F2115	Pinpoint impact → violent spherical/arc expansion → rolling outer tongues
Aftermath	Charred ground, cooling molten seams, ash and sparse embers	#FFB02E → #C74719 → #541411 → #171313	Embers rise slowly while fissures contract/dim

#FF4A4A is never used; warning red remains visually exclusive.

Small screen accent	Local heat refraction directly behind projectile
Big	Peripheral heat haze + embers visibly pulled toward impact point before detonation

3. WATER — Domain and Flow

Fantasy: The battlefield stops being dry land and becomes part of the user's current.

Law applied to choreography: Water never truly stops at impact. The weapon shape is only the leading structure of a larger current; it bends, wraps, reconnects and flows onward.

For self-hunting skills, Water should therefore look almost inevitable: one current naturally finds the next enemy.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	Water blades with glass-clear aqua body, bright pressure edge and long curling water ribbons; still unmistakably blade-shaped
Sabre	A heavy crescent breaker-wave, thin bright crest at front and thick translucent body behind
Spear	A focused high-pressure water lance/jet, bright central pressure filament surrounded by a twisting cylindrical current
Five phases
Phase	Material / shape	Ramp, core → edge	Motion
Charge	Thin water ribbons rise from field/caster and orbit into spirals; concentric ripples	#F5FFFF → #A5F6FF → #43C7F4 → #2474C7	Continuous circulation; each typed letter tightens the vortex
Release	Water sheet folds sharply into weapon silhouette; bright pressure edge appears	#FFFFFF → #A9F7FF → #39BDEB → #2264B6	Sudden directional surge, but rear water catches up smoothly
Travel	Clear blue volume + bright pressure edge + thin foam/caustic details	#EFFFFF → #88EEFF → #25A9E8 → #164F9B	Curves subtly; ribbons reconnect; self-hunting trajectories arc between targets instead of snapping
Impact	Water wraps target first, then bursts outward as crest/spiral; secondary current continues beyond	#F7FFFF → #9DF4FF → #36BDEB → #2565A8	Wrap → compress → surge through; avoid simple spherical splash
Aftermath	Reflective wet film, shallow streams, foam curls, droplets	#A9F7FF → #48B9DA → #316F9B	Water drains toward low points / next target; ripples continue after attack has finished
World, foe, screen, audio
Item	Design
Field before	Ground gradually darkens/wets; thin reflective film flows outward from caster; circular ripples appear where no rain has fallen
Field after	Shallow pooled water, flowing channels connecting impact points, wet reflections, foam traces; gradually drains/fades
Foe hit	Target is pulled sideways into current, rotates/bends with flow, then expelled
Foe death	Silhouette collapses into curling water ribbons + foam + a few dark ink motes; liquid keeps flowing past where body stood
Small screen accent	One refractive ripple crosses a small screen region
Big	Slow caustic-light sweep across field + sparse diagonal rain/water streaks at periphery
Ultimate	Peripheral wall/ceiling of water temporarily frames battlefield; large low-frequency lens refraction passes across screen, never over word tags for long
Sound	pressure-water hiss + deep liquid surge + fast crest slap + jet transient + submerged low boom + fine droplets/bubbles in tail
40–100 px read test	A visibly curved continuous ribbon/wave with a bright crest keeps flowing after contact.
Cheap failure	Transparent cyan tubes, generic blue magic, too many bubbles, or crystalline sharp facets that make Water read as Ice. Water needs mass + continuity.

4. THUNDER — Judgment From Above

Fantasy: The weapon does not create the lightning; it passes sentence, and heaven executes it.

This distinction is critical. Otherwise Thunder becomes merely “purple electricity attached to Sword/Sabre/Spear.”

Law applied to choreography: horizontal weapon motion marks, aims or sentences; the decisive power answers vertically from the sky.

Weapon material
Family	Projectile becomes
Sword	A dark/violet judgment blade with tiny white electrical edge; sword trajectory leaves a target mark, then vertical bolt answers
Sabre	A broad violet-white crescent of ionized air that paints a curved execution zone; descending strikes land across the crescent
Spear	An ultra-thin forward leader/locator stroke; at endpoint or along its path, a massive vertical lightning spear falls from sky
Five phases
Phase	Material / shape	Ramp, core → edge	Motion
Charge	Sparse electrical threads crawl upward toward sky; circular seal/halo builds above caster rather than around feet	#FFFFFF → #EFE5FF → #B68CFF → #6339DA	Upward leaders; brief branching, then branches collapse back into main line
Release	Weapon flashes as a thin violet-white designation line	#FFFFFF → #EADFFF → #A778FF → #5C35D0	Weapon motion finishes first; ~very short dramatic gap; then heaven responds
Travel	Weapon component is fast and thin; major energy is not a slow lightning projectile	#FFFFFF → #DFD5FF → #9D72F0 → #5130B5	Leader streak races horizontally/curves; charge climbs skyward from struck target
Impact	Thick, nearly straight sky-to-ground trunk, with branches appearing only after main trunk	#FFFFFF → #F0E7FF → #B98DFF → #5530CB	Instant vertical strike → one-frame exposure → delayed branching → thunder shock ring
Aftermath	Violet charge veins, small smoking pinholes, bright Lichtenberg-like ground lines	#DCCAFF → #8E62D7 → #47327F	Residual arcs jump infrequently between nearby debris/targets and then die
World, foe, screen, audio
Item	Design
Field before	Tiny violet pinpricks/seals appear under sentenced targets; dust lifts; vertical leaders crawl upward
Field after	Branching dark scorch lines radiating from strike points, tiny smoking craters, occasional residual arc
Foe hit	Rigid upward jerk / complete freeze for one frame, body becomes white silhouette, then violent downward collapse
Foe death	White-violet crack runs vertically through dark silhouette → body converts into sparse violet sparks and black wisps
Small screen accent	Very short exposure spike synchronized exactly with bolt
Big	Sky/upper screen briefly darkens; narrow vertical white-violet flash band tears downward
Ultimate	1–2 frame near-monochrome or near-black silence frame → enormous vertical judgment → pale retinal afterimage; retain violet/white rather than Acheron's red
Sound	pre-strike ion hiss + electrical intake → dry cannon-like crack → very short silence → large thunder body/sub → crackling aftertail
40–100 px read test	A vertical bolt visibly comes from outside the top of the screen after the weapon marks the target.
Cheap failure	Random branching purple lightning covering everything continuously. Branches become visual noise and remove the feeling that one specific target has been judged.
