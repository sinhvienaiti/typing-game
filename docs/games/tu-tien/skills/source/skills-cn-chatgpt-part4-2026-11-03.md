B. SELF-REVIEW BEFORE BUILD
The 5 strongest showpieces

1. WAT_U5_XUANHAI — Huyền Hải Khai Giới / Profound Sea Opens a Realm

This is probably the best single portfolio skill in the entire catalog. The floor becoming a transparent impossible ocean, followed by a creature shadow larger than the battlefield, communicates xianxia scale instantly at the fixed camera. It also gives the cut-in a genuine reveal payoff rather than merely showing the hero's face. Its strongest quality is that the monster is not “summoned in front of the hero”; it was apparently under the world all along.

Keep almost unchanged.

2. SWD_S4_TINGZHAO — Vạn Phong Thính Chiếu / Myriad Edges Hear the Edict

This best satisfies the owner's original demand. Hundreds of controlled weapons over almost the entire map, autonomously choosing beasts while the player continues typing, is exactly the sort of cultivation authority the old catalog lacked.

More importantly, it is still practical: your High tier already supports hundreds of instanced flying weapons.

This should be one of the first vertical-slice skills built.

3. ICE_U5_GUIJI — Hàn Thiên Quy Tịch / Cold Heaven Returns to Silence

This is the most aesthetically distinctive ultimate because it resists the temptation to compete through particle count. A battlefield with 100+ moving beasts suddenly becoming completely still and almost silent may feel more terrifying than another explosion.

It also gives Ice a real philosophical identity: not “blue damage,” but cessation.

Keep. Protect the silence; do not add a giant ice explosion at the end.

4. FIR_U5_YIXING — Nhất Tinh Phần Thiên / One Spark Burns Heaven

The tiny-white-star → map-sized consequence has the strongest scale contrast in the catalog. It captures what makes 佛怒火莲 compelling without copying the Fire Lotus itself. The moment where all other flames go out is particularly important because it makes the player's brain look at the tiny object.

Keep, but resist making the charge bigger. The spark should remain absurdly small.

5. ERT_U5_WANYUE — Vạn Nhạc Thành Chân / Ten Thousand Peaks Become Real

The flat painted/illusory landscape becoming actual three-dimensional mountain mass feels genuinely cultivation-fantasy rather than like an MMO Earth spell. It also distinguishes Earth from Zhongli-style “meteor = rock.”

Keep, but the visual transition from 2D/ghost contour → solid mountain must be the hero moment.

Weakest / most overlapping skills

The catalog is much stronger than the original one, but 66 skills still contain repetition.

MET_A0_GENGMANG, SWD_A0_HANMANG, SPR_A0_LONGYA, WAT_A0_SHUIZHEN, ICE_A0_SHUANGZHEN

These are mechanically appropriate basics, but on paper they are all variations of:

tiny precise ranged hit → small reaction.

That is acceptable for low-noise auto attacks, but five separate expensive bespoke VFX implementations would be wasteful.

I would use one shared technical system—ray/curve + element-specific shader/reaction—and differentiate mainly through timing, trail shape and death language.

SPR_S4_JIULONG vs FIR_S4_LIHUO

This is the clearest high-tier overlap.

Both are:

multiple serpentine mythical entities independently travel around the whole field and hunt packs.

Spear needs its nine spear-dragons because the long-axis “dragon thrust” is highly legible.

Fire should change.

I would cut the serpent body from FIR_S4_LIHUO. Keep the eight ritual seals, but have them fire autonomous living fire-stars that teleport from seal to seal and dive onto prey, so Fire becomes formation/network hunting rather than another dragon/serpent swarm.

That immediately separates it from Spear.

ERT_S3_DIMAI, WOD_S3_LIUYING, ICE_S3_SHUANGYING

We now have too many element dashes whose core is:

disappear → move 11–13 m → leave an elemental trail → reform.

The visuals differ, but the gameplay would blur together.

I would keep:

Wood S3, because leaving a living willow shell that snares enemies has tactical identity.
Thunder S3, because instantaneous thunder displacement is culturally and visually strong.
Spear S3, because the dash itself is an offensive thrust line.

Then reconsider Earth and Ice.

For Earth, I would replace the dash with a guard/terrain anchor that makes the hero impossible to displace and raises a half-ring cliff when struck.

For Ice, I would replace the dash with a short freeze-frame counter: the attacking beast's motion freezes while the cultivator calmly steps one body-width aside, then the stored motion resumes and cracks the attacker.

Those would improve the overall roster substantially.

THN_S4_TIANYAN vs SAB_S4_WUTAO

Mechanically both are invisible/remote judgments selecting clusters across the map.

Their visual languages are different—sky-eye lightning versus spontaneous execution planes—but the underlying player experience could feel similar.

I would keep both, but enforce a strict behavior difference:

Sabre: slow, large, highly lethal cluster executions every ~0.3 s.
Thunder: much faster repeated individual/mini-chain verdicts, almost machine-gun celestial judgment.

Thunder should feel like frequency; Sabre should feel like inevitability.

MET_U5_FAXIANG

This is visually large, but compared with Water, Fire, Ice and Earth it is slightly generic:

giant celestial humanoid appears and strikes.

“Giant dharma avatar” is powerful xianxia language, but it is also one of the easiest ideas to make look like generic fantasy/anime.

I would not cut Metal Ultimate, but I would change its centerpiece.

Instead of a humanoid palm being the main event, make the manifestation only a faint background presence. The true climax should be:

for one moment, every visible edge in the world becomes perfectly aligned toward the enemies—rocks, architecture, weapon edges, shadows, even terrain cracks—and then one metallic tone causes all of those lines to cut simultaneously.

That would make Metal much more original.

What I would cut or merge before production

If production started now, I would make five changes before anyone builds final VFX:

Merge the technical implementation of the precision basics rather than creating five unrelated systems. Their art stays different; their code does not.

Redesign Fire S4 away from roaming serpent/dragon bodies. Preserve the eight ritual seals but use seal-to-seal autonomous fire-stars or miniature furnace gates.

Replace Earth S3 with a mountain-anchor guard/counter. Earth should resist displacement rather than joining the game's already crowded dash family.

Replace Ice S3 with a stillness/time-like freeze counter. It would reinforce Ice's unique theme far better than another elemental dash.

Rework Metal Ultimate's focal action from “giant avatar attacks” into “the entire world's edges become weapons.” Keep the celestial manifestation only as framing.

After those changes, the schools have much cleaner identities:

Sword = command.
Sabre = execution.
Spear = vector.
Metal = edge / hardness.
Wood = life / ecology.
Water = domain / flow.
Fire = compression / catastrophe.
Earth = mass / reality.
Thunder = heavenly judgment.
Wind = motion / atmosphere.
Ice = cessation / silence.

That is the version I would take into prototype rather than building all 66 exactly as written.
