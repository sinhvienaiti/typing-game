# Asset search brief: ready-made VFX and icons (for a web search assistant)

Written 31/10/2026 for the owner to hand to ChatGPT (or any search assistant). Please search the web and return candidates in the table format at the end. Do not buy or download anything; the owner decides.

## The games

Three browser games (WebGL / three.js, shipped as a web build, sold or shown commercially):
- **Dragonhold**: a lane defense with dragons. Painted, warm, stylized 3D world seen from a tilted top-down camera (about 50–60° down). Reference level: Kingdom Rush, Clash Royale, Hades.
- **Last Line**: a sci-fi corridor survival. Dark steel and graphite, hazard orange, electric cyan, a black hole in the sky. Reference level: Warframe, Destiny menus, Hades HUD readability.
- **Wordwoven**: a village defense, jade, gold leaf and bronze, East Asian folk style.

## Licence rules (hard requirements)

- Commercial use allowed in a game, and the files may ship **inside the built game** (not redistributed as raw assets).
- **Engine-agnostic**: usable in a custom WebGL engine, not only in Unity or Unreal. Unity Asset Store and Fab packs are fine only if their licence allows use outside that engine; say what the licence says.
- CC0, royalty-free, or a one-time paid licence. CC-BY is acceptable if attribution is the only condition (we keep a credits file).
- No packs that are themselves AI-generated or that forbid use with AI tools in the pipeline; no "personal use only", no "editorial only".
- Give the exact licence name and a link to its text for every candidate.

## Formats we can use

- Sprite sheets or PNG sequences **with alpha** (straight or premultiplied, say which), or video with alpha (ProRes 4444, WebM VP9 alpha).
- Engine packs are fine **if they include the flipbook textures** (we rebuild the particle behaviour ourselves). Packs that are only particle-system definitions with tiny generic textures are not useful.
- Frame size 256 px or more per frame (512 preferred for big explosions), 16–64 frames, 24–60 fps source.
- Bonus: separate layers (fire body, glow, smoke, sparks), emissive/temperature or motion-vector maps.

## Category 1 (most urgent): stylized dragon fire hit, Dragonhold

What we need, in this order:
1. **Big fireball impact / explosion**: hand-painted or stylized (not photoreal), rolling fire with volume, a small white-yellow core, saturated orange body, deep red edges, cooling into smoke. 0.6–1.2 s. Must read on light green grass and on dark ground. Something like the Clash Royale Fireball impact, Kingdom Rush "Rain of Fire" explosion, or Hades fire hits.
2. **Stylized smoke puffs** that rise and dissipate, two- or three-tone lit (light on top, darker underneath), separate from the fire.
3. **Ground scorch / burn mark decal** seen from above, dark brown with glowing ember edges, PNG with alpha.
4. **Shockwave or dust ring** on the ground, and **sparks / embers** sheets.
5. Nice to have: a **flame breath / fire jet** (for the dragon's mouth), a **looping fireball projectile**, and a **meteor strike** with a tall column or mushroom explosion (for a "Sky Fire" ultimate).

Good search terms: "stylized fire explosion sprite sheet", "hand painted VFX fire flipbook", "toon explosion flipbook pack", "Cartoon FX", "Epic Toon FX", "stylized VFX texture pack", "fireball impact sprite sheet 512", "realtime VFX flipbook pack stylized". Sites to check: itch.io, Unity Asset Store, Fab (Unreal), CraftPix, GameDev Market, ArtStation Marketplace, Gumroad (VFX artists sell flipbook packs), Kenney, OpenGameArt, and any CC0 VFX libraries.

## Category 2: game UI icons, Last Line

Icons are drawn at **20–32 px** in menus and the HUD, so they must be **bold, simple silhouettes** that still read at that size, in a consistent style. We need a set that covers: settings gear, sound speaker (on and muted), music, keyboard, crosshair or target, kills, accuracy, timer or stopwatch, warning or hazard triangle, play or resume, next stage, profile or pilot helmet, ammo or magazine, rift or portal, steadiness or balance, a new-item badge, ship or starfighter (top-down), gun silhouettes (beam cannon, rail gun, mortar, shotgun, pulse rifle).
- A sci-fi set in steel, orange and cyan is ideal; a clean monochrome silhouette set that we can colour is also good.
- Search terms: "sci-fi game UI icon pack", "game icons silhouette pack", "HUD icon set sci-fi". Check game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, 4,000+ silhouettes), itch.io, Unity Asset Store, CraftPix, GameDev Market.

## Category 3 (lower priority): settings panel parts

Slider (groove, fill, knob), list rows, close button, panel frames in a fantasy gold-and-stone style (Dragonhold) and a jade-and-gold East Asian style (Wordwoven), as nine-slice PNGs.
